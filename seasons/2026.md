# 2026 season

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Event | Shell Eco-marathon United States 2026, Indianapolis Motor Speedway (April) |
| Category | Prototype Battery Electric |
| Result | **6th place**, ~310 km/kWh |

## What we built

- **Motor driver:** field-oriented control (FOC) with Hall sensors on a Teensy 4.1 (ARM Cortex-M7 at 600 MHz), with a throttle-to-current curve.
- **Telemetry:** ESP32 board of our own design; GPS, IMU, Pitot tube and driver data; extended Kalman filter with adaptive trust; microSD logging; live link WiFi → backend → WebSockets → dashboard; cockpit display with map, target speed and pit messages.
- **Post-race analysis:** math channels for driver efficiency, vehicle performance and losses.

## Towards 2027

Shell Eco-marathon United States 2027 (Indianapolis, April 6–10): current sampling synchronized with the PWM, sensorless start-up, a new motor with a clutch, precision energy measurement and a predictive co-pilot.
