# Mechanics

A three-wheeled, ultra-light prototype built around one idea: lose as little energy as possible to rolling, air and weight. Two steered wheels at the front, one driven wheel at the back, a closed shell over the driver.

| Aspect | Detail |
|---|---|
| Layout | Three wheels: a front pair with trapezoidal steering (partial Ackermann, the inner wheel turns more, geometry measured in CAD) and a single driven wheel centred at the back |
| Size (2026 car) | About 2.7 m long, 0.8 m wide and 0.8 m tall, from the CAD assembly |
| Body | Monocoque shell over the chassis; the shell is also the basis of the x-ray model on this site |
| Brakes | Hydraulic bicycle brakes on all three wheels (Shimano Zee calipers) |
| Powertrain (2026) | A 350 W hub motor inside the rear wheel, without a clutch |
| Mass | Lightest car of the 2024 Americas competition at 30 kg; 31 kg in the 2023 debut |
| Design flow | SolidWorks; part numbers by subsystem (chassis, steering, brakes, powertrain, body, electronics and sensor mounts); a bill of materials checked automatically; each approved revision released as STEP, drawings PDF and DXF |

## History

### 2027 · Chain drive with a clutch and measured losses (in progress)

- **New powertrain:** a motor of about 500 W at 48 V with chain drive and a clutch (freewheel), so the motor stops dragging while the car coasts. In the team's energy model this architecture spends about 20 % less energy than the hub motor.
- **Measure instead of assume:** coast-down tests for rolling resistance and drag, plus weighing, tyre pressure, alignment and free-spin checks.
- **Autonomy kit mounts:** supports for the steering and brake actuators on the same car.

### 2026 · Hub motor in the rear wheel

- A 350 W hub motor inside the rear wheel, without a clutch: while the car coasts the rotor keeps turning and drags.

### 2024 · Lightest car of the competition

- 30 kg, the lightest car of the whole Shell Eco-marathon Americas 2024 ([2024 season](../seasons/2024.md)).

### 2023 · Debut at 31 kg

- Second-lightest car in the Americas and lightest in Mexico ([2023 season](../seasons/2023.md)).
